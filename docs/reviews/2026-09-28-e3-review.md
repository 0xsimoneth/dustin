## Method

Two reviews ran, each with the `code-review` workflow in four layers; its edge layers used the `review-edge-case-hunter` skill:

1. **E2-S3 and E2-S4, round 3**, over the second-round fixes (`8c890b9..953fbd7`, 4,545 diff lines): a Blind Hunter that saw the diff only; two Edge Case Hunters with read access to the repository (executor; CLI and receipt); an Acceptance Auditor against stories 2-3 and 2-4, the 2026-09-27 review and the canonical decisions.
2. **The closing review**, over the whole session (`c7be815..562d542`, 89 commits; 5,098 lines of source diff and 12,187 of tests): a Blind Hunter over the source diff; three Edge Case Hunters (executor, sponsor and configuration; planner, inspector and fixture builder; CLI, receipt and evidence tooling); an Acceptance Auditor against stories E3-S1 to E3-S7 and E2-S3/E2-S4, the SOW, the canonical decisions, the PRD and the committed evidence.

Most findings were proved with throwaway probe tests in isolated worktrees outside the repository before they were fixed; every code fix came with a test that failed first. Protocol facts were checked through the Raven MCP against developers.stellar.org and against the stellar-core source.

No layer of either review found a major defect in the Epic 3 code. Round 3 found one major defect in the round-2 fixes (R3-1).
