## Dev Notes

- Protocol facts, and where they come from. A claim needs the claimant as the operation's source account and a trustline for an asset other than XLM, and "there is no recovery mechanism for a claimable balance in general" (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/guides/transactions/claimable-balances#claim-claimable-balance, #example). Creating a claimable balance raises the creator's minimum balance by one base reserve per claimant (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/learn/fundamentals/transactions/list-of-operations#create-claimable-balance; observed: the plain issuer's `num_sponsoring` was 3 for a one-claimant and a two-claimant balance). The claimant query is the one js-stellar-sdk's `ClaimableBalanceCallBuilder.claimant()` builds (https://github.com/stellar/js-stellar-sdk/blob/master/src/horizon/claimable_balances_call_builder.ts). Available balance is balance minus minimum balance minus selling liabilities (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/guides/transactions/sponsored-reserves#effect-on-minimum-balance). A buy offer's price is "1 unit of buying in terms of selling", and a manage-sell-offer amount of 0 deletes an offer (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/learn/fundamentals/transactions/list-of-operations#manage-buy-offer, #manage-sell-offer). A passive order does not take a counter order at the same price (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/learn/fundamentals/liquidity-on-stellar-sdex-liquidity-pools#passive-order). A path payment that would cross one of its own offers fails with PATH_PAYMENT_STRICT_SEND_OFFER_CROSS_SELF (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/data/apis/horizon/api-reference/errors/result-codes/operation-specific/path-payment-strict-send); Horizon spells it `op_cross_self` (observed). An account can be created with startingBalance 0 when another account sponsors its reserves (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/build/guides/transactions/create-account#create-an-account-1, CAP-33). A testnet reset clears every ledger entry and restarts from the genesis ledger (https://developers.stellar.org/docs/networks#testnet-and-futurenet-data-reset); Horizon keeps a merged account's operations (day-1 experiment 11) and answers 404 for the operations of an account it has never seen (observed on 2026-09-28).
- Experiments before the recipe change, with throwaway Friendbot keys (2026-09-28, not tests): Horizon lists a buy offer of 0.0000002 XLM at 2 OFB per XLM as selling 0.0000004 OFB at `price_r` 1/2; an account with the three offers and 3.5000010 XLM has spendable 0; Horizon's strict-send answer for 0.0000003 OFA is 0.0000003 XLM through the account's own offer, and none for OFB; a manageBuyOffer that takes an offer fails with `op_low_reserve` when the taker cannot afford an offer that might rest, while a strict-receive path payment takes it without one; the cancellation of the taken offer fails with `op_offer_not_found`; a sponsorship sandwich creates a 0 XLM account with `num_sponsored` 3.
- Recipe (canonical decision 3): the new cases are variants of the one `edge` profile, and the ten earlier variants are unchanged, so `test/fixtures/horizon/edge/` still describes what the recipe builds for them. The plain issuer starts with 3 XLM instead of 2 (a helper, not a variant) for the claimant's balances and the X-08 counterparty. No two offers of the build cross: none sells what another buys (a recipe test). X-16 is a setup inside its live test rather than a variant: its account needs a reserve sponsor inside a sandwich, and the edge build's accounts are all created by the fee sponsor.
- Older manifests: a manifest lists the variants it was built with, so `readAnyManifest` requires public keys for the helpers and the listed variants only, and `dustin fixture verify` checks and reads only those. The verifier appends the new checks after the old ones, so a ten-variant build keeps its 62 check ids in their order; a current build has 78.
- Offline vectors: `test/fixtures/horizon/edge-e4/` is the complete recording of the CLI build `edge-20260928T205933Z-3a366d` (56 files, all 13 variants); the keys stayed in `.fixture/` (gitignored), and the recording holds no seed and none of the build's secret keys (checked before the commit). The build harness of the fixture tests (`rekeyedEdgeHorizon`) fakes a build of the current recipe from it. `DUSTIN_RECORD=1` on `test/testnet/edge.test.ts` now writes that directory. The synthesized empty claimant pages for the messy, pool-share and ten-variant edge vectors carry a `synthesized` field that says so; the messy vectors are of the builder's baseline fixture, which was not read live.
- Test files outside the ownership of this story: the offline executor twins of X-03, X-07 and X-08 are in `test/unit/fixture/edge-e4-close.test.ts`, because `test/unit/execute/` belongs to the executor's owner in this session. The fake ledger returns no result XDR, so `recovery.mergedXlm` stays null offline; those tests read the destination's balance instead.
- Reset detection is wired into `dustin fixture verify`, the one code path in `src` that consumes a manifest. `scripts/evidence-plan.mjs` also reads a manifest, only to pass its accounts to `dustin plan`, which reports a missing account as `ACCOUNT_MISSING`; that script is not this story's file.
